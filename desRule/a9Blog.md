# RHODES_ISLAND_INTELLIGENCE_REPORTS_RULES.md

# DESIGN OBJECTIVE

Transform the Blog page into the Rhodes Island Intelligence Reports System.

The page should resemble an internal intelligence and reporting platform used by Rhodes Island personnel.

Users are not reading blog posts.

Users are reviewing operational reports, technical analyses, research documents, and intelligence records.

This is:

* Intelligence Reports
* Research Publications
* Technical Analysis Records
* Operational Logs
* Documentation Reports

This is NOT:

* Personal Blog
* Medium Clone
* News Website
* Marketing Content Platform
* Social Publishing Platform
